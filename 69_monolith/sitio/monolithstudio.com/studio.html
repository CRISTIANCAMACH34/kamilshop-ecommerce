The transformation
of ideas into art

The tattoo process is a collaboration between you and your tattoo artist that will last forever. We bring together the world's most unique and talented tattoo artists to work with you in our tattoo studio in Brooklyn, NYC.

Artistic
Philosophy

As Monolith Studio, we believe in pushing the boundaries of creativity with our long years of tattoo experience, inspired by each of our collectors we choose to work with.

Core Values

When working with our tattoo collectors, we focus on three elements: connection, uniqueness, and creative innovation.

Connection

We value the connection our tattoos and designs will establish with the tattoo collector and the relationship they will continue to have throughout their lives, and we consider this to be one of the most important issues throughout the process.

Uniqueness

We believe that every design and tattoo we make should be a unique work of art.

Creative

We believe that we should push the boundaries of creativity so that each tattoo is a unique design and idea that has never been tried before.

Commitment
to Quality

For us, each tattoo should be a unique work of art, so we decide which collectors we will work with by evaluating only the best ideas, but in addition to this, we work with the best tattooists in the world to ensure that every tattoo made at Monolith Studio is technically of the best standards and we strive to become a better artist every day by deeply studying all innovations and technical details.
