About the artist
Maxime Plescia-Buchi (he/they) is a designer, tattooer and entrepreneur. Born in Lausanne, Switzerland in 1978. Currently based in NY, USA. Founder of Swiss Typefaces, Sang Bleu Tattoo, Sang Bleu Magazine, TTTISM Magazine, , Novembre magazine. Maxime studied psychology and human sciences at the University of Lausanne and Design at ECAL, University of Arts, Lausanne. He then lived in Zurich, Paris and London where he worked as graphic designer for studios and magazines such as NORM, North Design, Self Service Magazine, Arena Homme + then opened his first tattoo studio. Maxime also taught regularly at the ECAL, HEAD (Geneva) and the Basel Fashion School. Maxime apprenticed as tattoo artist under Filip Leu in Lausanne.
Corporate clients include: Hublot. McQ, Allies of Skin, Virgin Galactic. Mugler, Swiss Confederation, Swiss Post, Dubai Public Transports, City of Stockholm, Michael Kors, Rick Owens, Balenciaga, Vogue Turkey, Esquire Magazine, Ebay, Rolls Royce, Sky Television, Android, McQueen.
Other clients include: Isamaya Ffrench, Matthew Williams, Kanye West, Virgil Abloh, Frank Ocean, FKA Twigs Mabe, Adam Lambert, Nicola Formichetti, Marcelo Burlon, David Kleinberg, Josephine Skriver, Pierre Biver, Maxime Ballesteros, Alvaro Barrington, Tiona Nekkia McClodden.
