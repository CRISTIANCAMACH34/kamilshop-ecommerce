Privacy
Policy

This document explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you interact with our studio and services. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring that your personal data is handled with the utmost care and respect. By using our services, you consent to the practices outlined in this policy.

General

This Policy outlines the procedures for Monolith Studio's processing of personal data provided by customers using the monolithstudio.com website and its services (hereinafter referred to as Users and Website, respectively). It details the types of personal data collected, the purposes for which this data is used, Monolith Studio's interactions with third parties, the security measures in place to protect personal data, the conditions for accessing personal data, and contact information for Users regarding access, modifications, blocking, or removal of their personal data, as well as addressing any questions related to personal data protection practices.
Monolith Studio respects the confidentiality of all individuals visiting the monolithstudio.com website without exception, as well as those who use its services. We are committed to protecting the confidentiality of personal data (information or a set of information about an individual who is identified or can be specifically identified). Our goal is to create and ensure the most comfortable conditions for each user to use the services of our Website.
The text of this Policy is available to users online at monolithstudio.com/privacy-policy. If you disagree with the terms of the Policy, you must immediately stop using the Website.

Terminology

Personal Data: Any information relating directly or indirectly to an identified or identifiable natural person (subject of personal data).
Processing of Personal Data: Any action or set of actions performed with or without the use of automated tools on personal data. This includes collection, recording, systematization, accumulation, storage, clarification (updating, modification), retrieval, usage, transfer (distribution, provision, access), anonymization, blocking, deletion, and destruction of personal data.
Automated Processing of Personal Data: Processing personal data using computer technology.
Dissemination of Personal Data: Actions aimed at disclosing personal data to an indefinite number of persons.
Providing Personal Data: Actions aimed at disclosing personal data to a specific person or a defined group of persons.
Blocking Personal Data: Suspension of personal data processing (unless the processing is necessary to clarify personal data).
Use of Personal Data: Actions (operations) with personal data performed by Monolith Studio to make decisions or perform other actions that result in legal consequences concerning the subject of personal data or other persons, or otherwise affect the rights and freedoms of the subject of personal data or other persons.
Destruction of Personal Data: Actions resulting in the irreversible elimination of personal data content in the information system and/or the destruction of the physical carriers of personal data.
Site: A collection of information, texts, graphic elements, design, images, photos, video materials, and other intellectual property, as well as computer programs, contained in an information system that ensures the availability of such information on the Internet at the address monolithstudio.com.
Cookies: Data automatically transmitted to Monolith Studio during the use of the Site via the software installed on the User's device. This includes IP address, geographic location, browser information, type of operating system, technical characteristics of the equipment and software used by the User, and the date and time of access to the Site. Cookies can be permanent (persistent cookies) and remain on the user's device until deleted or temporary (session cookies), which are stored only until the browser is closed. Additionally, cookies are classified as primary (set directly by the visited Site) and third-party (set by other sites).
IP Address: A numeric label used by computers on a network to identify the computer each time they access the Internet.

Use of Cookies

Monolith Studio is committed to ensuring that your experience on our Site is as comfortable as possible. To achieve this, we analyze user behavior, preferences, and interests using cookies. This analysis helps us improve your interaction with the Site, determine the most user-friendly interface, and enhance navigation.
According to the International Chamber of Commerce classification, Monolith Studio uses the following categories of cookies:
Strictly Necessary Cookies: These cookies are essential for navigating the Site and using certain services, such as accessing secure pages, registering, and logging in. They also remember your actions on previous pages during the same session.
Performance Cookies: These cookies collect aggregated information about how the Site is used. The data, stored on the user's device between browser sessions, includes metrics such as time spent on the Site, most frequently visited pages, and the effectiveness of advertising and marketing campaigns. The information collected is used for statistical and analytical purposes and may be shared with third parties authorized by the web resource.
Functional Cookies: Functional cookies save settings or configurations stored on the user's device between browser sessions. This includes information like username, profile photo, comments, site language, and location. These cookies also enable users to watch videos, participate in interactive activities, and interact with social networks. Some data may be shared with third parties authorized by the web resource.
Targeting Cookies: Targeting cookies provide content that may be of interest to the user, stored on the user's device between browser sessions. This includes tracking recommended text, graphics, audio, and video material, managing targeted advertising, evaluating advertising campaign effectiveness, and tracking user visits to other resources. This information may be shared with other parties, such as media clients, advertisers, agencies, and business partners, to provide quality targeted advertising.
Monolith Studio ensures that all information collected through cookies is used to enhance your experience and improve our services. We adhere to strict privacy and security measures to protect your data.

Monolith Studio processes Users' personal data solely for the following purposes:

Processing of Personal Data: The processing and storage of provided personal data are conducted in data centers where the equipment ensuring the Site's functionality is located. Personal data may be processed and stored in the Personal Data Base or in a separate table within the Site Database.
Personal data is processed using automated systems, except when non-automated processing is necessary to fulfill legal requirements.

Protection of personal information

Monolith Studio takes necessary and sufficient legal, organizational, and technical measures to protect the information provided by Users from unauthorized or accidental access, destruction, modification, blocking, copying, and distribution, as well as from other illegal actions by third parties. However, despite these efforts, Monolith Studio cannot guarantee absolute security against all potential threats beyond its control.
Monolith Studio employs standard security practices such as encrypted communications, secure premises, firewall systems, and password protection systems to ensure the confidentiality of personal data.
Access to information and personal data is restricted to authorized employees who have committed to maintaining the confidentiality of such information and data according to Monolith Studio's requirements.
The Site may contain links to other websites (for informational purposes only). If you follow a link to other websites, this Policy will not apply to those sites. Monolith Studio recommends that you familiarize yourself with the privacy and personal data policies of each site before transferring personal data by which you can be identified.

User rights

Users have the following rights regarding their personal data:
Provide Personal Data: Users can voluntarily provide personal data for processing under the terms specified in this Policy.
Delete Personal Data: Users can delete their personal data by editing the "Personal Data" or "My Interests" sections in their personal account.
Request Data Clarification: Users can request the Individual Entrepreneur to clarify, block, or destroy their personal data if it is incomplete, outdated, inaccurate, illegally obtained, or not necessary for the stated purpose of processing. Users can also take legal measures to protect their rights.
Receive Information: Users have the right to receive, no later than thirty calendar days from the date of the request (unless otherwise provided by law), a response regarding whether their personal data is being processed and receive the content of such personal data.
Object to Data Processing: Users can present a reasoned demand to the data owner with an objection to the processing of their personal data.
Legal Remedies: Users can apply legal remedies in case of violation of personal data protection laws, including filing a complaint with a supervisory authority.
Update and Withdraw Consent: To update, access, amend, block, or delete personal data, or to withdraw consent to the processing of personal data provided under this Policy, Users can contact Monolith Studio via email at hello@monolithstudio.com.
Submit Requests: Users can send requests and requirements, including consent to process personal data, in writing to the address of Monolith Studio (as listed in section 11 of the Policy) or via email (from the email address specified during registration or in the contract as an authorized email address) to hello@monolithstudio.com.
