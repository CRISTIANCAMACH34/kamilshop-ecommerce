Terms of Use

This document provides essential information about the terms and conditions governing your use of our studio, artists, and related services. By accessing or using our services, you agree to adhere to these terms, which outline your rights, responsibilities, and limitations. Please read this document carefully to understand how we operate, the services we offer, and how we manage interactions between our users and our studio.

Booking and scheduling

Deposits are non-refundable and are required to secure your appointment date and time, as well as to compensate the artist for their design work before the session. This deposit will be applied toward the final cost of your tattoo.If you need to reschedule your appointment, please inform us at least three (3) or more days in advance to rearrange your appointment. Providing a valid reason for rescheduling is crucial. Without a valid reason, you may forfeit your deposit.
You can reschedule your appointment up to 2 times. On the third reschedule, you will lose your deposit and need to pay a new deposit.Deposits are valid for up to 6 months from the original appointment date for rescheduling. After this period, you will lose your deposit and need a new one to book again.

Appointment day

Please bring a valid government-issued ID on the day of your appointment. This ID is necessary to confirm your identity. Failure to present a valid ID will result in the loss of your appointment and deposit.

Hygiene and sterilization practices

All Monolith Studio artist and staff are extensively trained in the standards set by State and Industry Law. We not only meet these standards but exceed them. All stations are meticulously cleaned and disinfected before and after each session. In consultation with the New York State Health Department, we've ensured all surfaces (walls, floors, furniture, equipment) meet sterility standards.
Our disinfection and sterilization procedures are continuously reviewed and improved. All equipment is single-use and checked for expiration dates. Items that come into direct contact with skin are safely disposed of after each session. Sterile equipment is unpacked in front of the client. Each artist’s station is organized and optimized for workflow.
Artists and staff wear washable work clothing.
A sterile workstation with single-use supplies is provided for each artist for every appointment.
Both the client and artist properly sanitize all body areas subject to contact or exposure.
Workstations are properly spaced and separated from high-traffic areas.
All furnishings, floors, and walls are washable and easy to disinfect.
Multiple disinfectants are located close to workstations.
All direct surfaces used in the tattooing process are covered with a sterilization barrier, which is disposed of after each session. The surface is then sterilized for the next session.
We require clients to sign a detailed consent form, informing them of all potential concerns and clauses. We strive to accommodate each client’s needs, especially if there are any health concerns.

Payment

Artists set their own prices and provide estimates based on size, placement, complexity, and other factors.
Artists will estimate a general price range for your consideration. Unless there are significant changes to the design or unforeseen circumstances, you will not be billed more than the highest amount of the estimated range.
A non-refundable deposit is required to book an appointment and secure the date and time. Billing begins at the agreed start time of the appointment, regardless of your arrival time. Failure to arrive within a reasonable time frame or not showing up will result in the automatic forfeiture of your deposit.
In case of uploading your image through the personal account of the Site - royalty-free consents to the use of this image for purposes not related to the identification of the User. The User undertakes not to provide photographs of third parties as an image of the User.
Significant design changes after booking may result in a cost change due to increased time estimates and may lead to forfeiture of your deposit and appointment. Please inform us at least three (3) or more days in advance if you are considering significant design changes.

Tattoo risks

A tattoo is a permanent piece of art. Currently, there is no known method to completely remove a tattoo. While tattoos are becoming more popular and accepted in general culture, they are not welcomed everywhere. It’s important to consider how tattoos will affect your perception, especially in certain professions or cultures.
A tattoo will always differ slightly from the drawing/stencil. Many factors, including skin type, tone, and body shape, contribute to this.
Complications, allergies, infections, and inflammations are possible risks. Despite our adherence to the highest hygiene and sanitation standards, there is always a risk of reaction. We strongly recommend consulting with a doctor or dermatologist to assess personal risks before scheduling an appointment.
Artists will provide their detailed aftercare procedure, but maintaining that regimen is up to you. If you have any questions or concerns along the way, please feel free to contact us.
In very rare situations, skin can be prone to a “blow-out.” A “blow-out” occurs when the skin cannot retain ink properly, making it appear like ink on blotting paper. While this is harmless, it’s a risk that cannot be prevented. Please consult with a doctor or dermatologist if this is of personal concern.
Research the tattoo aging process, possible skin conditions, or any other concerns you may have. We recommend consulting your dermatologist if necessary. We are happy to provide as much information as we can, but it’s important to be well-informed before your appointment.

Our commitment

Our studio is clean and organized.
Our staff is friendly and helpful.
We provide a comprehensive consent form with written information and agreements for completion.
Clients are fully informed about all risks involved in the tattoo process.
Clients are asked to provide a government-issued ID.
